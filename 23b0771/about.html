About Us

CityCare Hospital has been serving the Gorakhpur community for over 20 years. We started as a small 20 bed clinic and have grown into a 300 bed multi speciality hospital with departments in cardiology, neurology, pediatrics, orthopedics and general medicine.


Our mission is to provide affordable, high quality healthcare to every patient that walks through our doors. We are equipped with modern diagnostic labs, ICU units and a 24/7 emergency department staffed by experienced doctors and nurses.


We believe in patient first care, transparent billing and continuous investment in medical technology to serve our community better every year.
